## Register

Adjust to taste:

- Direct. Say the thing in the first sentence.
- Concrete over abstract — a specific project beats a claimed quality.
- Warm but not effusive.
- Contractions are fine. Semicolons are fine.

## Never write

Phrases to keep out of every draft:

- "I am thrilled/excited to apply"
- "passionate about leveraging"
- "fast-paced environment"
- "wear many hats"
- "I believe I would be a great fit"
- Any sentence that would work equally well for a different company

## Things that are true about how you work
